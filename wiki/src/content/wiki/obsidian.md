Obsidian keeps notes as Markdown files on disk. A note links to another by writing its name in double brackets. The app can show a graph of those links, and a panel of [[backlink|backlinks]] for the note that is open.

The bracket syntax is older than the app. What it made ordinary, for a lot of people, is that the files stayed plain Markdown and the links were visible while editing, not only after a publish step.

This description is not on a review schedule, and product details move. The durable part is the part this wiki borrows: local Markdown, and links you can follow in both directions.
